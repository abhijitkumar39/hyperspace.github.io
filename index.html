The scrape

National brands in. Our own model out.

These are the sites the spiders actually ran. Click one. Fuel, parking, homes, and jobs all took the same path, and the clean rows were what we trained on.

  1. Collect Scrapy on a schedule. Rotating proxies when a brand started blocking. One project per site, not a one-off script.
  2. Clean Raw pages landed in MongoDB. ETL typed the prices, places, hours, and listing text an API could serve.
  3. Train Those tables were the corpus for an in-house LLM. It learned our prices and places, then answered from that store.

The model was not trained on a generic crawl. It learned our tables: a pump price that moved through the day, a parking bay that was still open, a home listing that changed, a job post that vanished. Ask where gas is cheaper, or which builder still has inventory, and the answer comes from that store.

The version of this that is actually mine sits in the third story: a Telegram bot on a local Ollama model.
