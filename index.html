Personal · a model I run

A sentence in. A reminder, or an answer, out.

This one is mine. A Telegram bot in front of a local model, Ollama running qwen3:4b on a Mac Mini. Tailscale is how I reach that machine, so the model stays at home and the chat still works from my phone.

I write to it the way I would write to a person. Ask it to schedule something and the model picks out the time and saves the reminder. Ask it a normal question and it answers. The typing mark appears the moment the message is sent, and stays up until the reply is ready.

Live search is the next piece, so a question about today can come back with something current instead of only what the model already knows.

The scrape

Rows in. Our own model out.

Fuel, parking, homes, and jobs took the same path. Spiders, then ETL, then an LLM trained on our tables. RAG answered from that store.

  1. Collect Scrapy on a schedule. Fuel, parking, homes, and jobs each had their own job.
  2. Clean Raw pages landed in MongoDB. ETL typed the prices, places, hours, and listing text.
  3. Train Those tables trained our own LLMs. RAG so an answer came back from our rows.

The model was trained on our tables. A price that moved through the day, a bay that was still open, a listing that changed. RAG retrieved those rows at question time, so the answer cited our store.
